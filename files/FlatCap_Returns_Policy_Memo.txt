Flat Cap Co. memo
From: Felix Bramble, Operations
Date: November 2, 2026
Subject: Returns are changing on December 1

Starting December 1, every return gets one of five reason codes: Fit, Color or pattern, Damaged, Wrong item, Changed mind. Support staff choose the code. Customers no longer type in their own reason.

Why: First-quarter returns rose 13%. "Fit not as expected" was the top reason at 38%, but reasons were logged so inconsistently that we couldn't tell what to fix.

What's new for customers: Returns for fit become a free exchange for a different size within 30 days, and we pay shipping both ways. All other returns stay refund-only with a $6 return label fee.

Staff action: Finish the 10-minute reason-code guide by November 24. Questions go to Lindsay Draper.

(Flat Cap Co. is a fictional company used for training.)
